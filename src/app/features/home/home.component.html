ABOUT ME

From physical infrastructure to software systems.

I graduated with a B.Tech in Civil Engineering from Sri Venkateswara College of Engineering (SVCE), Tirupati. My transition from physical infrastructure to software engineering was a deliberate pivot: shifting focus from concrete and structural foundations to the backend software layers that operate public safety and smart-city infrastructure.

Since June 2024, I have been working as an Associate Software Developer at Trinity Mobility in Bengaluru. My code operates inside mission-critical systems where reliability is non-negotiable: Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) for emergency numbers (Namma112 Karnataka and Maharashtra 112), Kerala's state disaster early-warning system (KaWaCHaM), and municipal centralized lighting infrastructure.

Alongside enterprise platforms, I independently built, tested, and deployed the commercial business website for LANS Lift ↗ end to end on AWS, managing everything from frontend development in Angular to DNS routing and cloud hosting.
